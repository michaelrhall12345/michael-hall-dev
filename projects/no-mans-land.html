No Man's

Third-person melee and ranged action game with stealth elements (codename "Project Midnight"), built in Unreal Engine 5. As Contract Gameplay Engineer at Lost Boys Interactive, I was the sole engineer on the overhaul of the core player combat and AI behind the investor demo's combat loop, delivered inside a two-month production window. After the demo shipped I stayed on for post-demo production, turning demo-scoped systems into durable architecture: a combat state harness, a weapon class hierarchy, and reusable components that the next wave of enemies and player abilities build on. Under deadline, the rule was targeted fixes over new structure; the deferred work got tracked and paid down once the demo was out.

// systems.built
01 — Camera
Soft Lock-On Camera
Soft lock-on that keeps the active threat framed without taking control away from the player, paired with motion warping so attacks close distance and align to the target. Toggle mode currently in progress.
02 — Combat
Player Combat Overhaul
Sole engineer on the rework of the player combat systems driving the demo's combat loop, alongside a full AI revamp. Rebuilt to be readable, tunable, and stable enough to put in front of investors.
03 — Arch
Combat State Harness
Post-demo state framework that player combat actions run through, giving new mechanics a consistent place to plug in. Currently being extended to block states.
04 — Arch
Weapon Class Hierarchy
Refactored melee and ranged weapons into a shared class hierarchy, pulling common behavior into base classes and moving ammo handling into its own component, so new weapons extend existing ones instead of duplicating logic.
05 — AI
Feral Soldier
The standard grunt archetype. Built to pressure the player in numbers and teach the combat loop, and the baseline every other enemy is tuned against.
06 — AI
Half Breed
A ranged harasser that punishes standing still and pulls the player's attention off melee threats, forcing them to prioritize targets and keep moving.
07 — AI
Werewolf Boss
The demo's boss encounter, with its own behavior and combat logic, built to test everything the player learned against the grunts and harassers.
08 — Component
Reusable Throwing Component
Generic throw component designed to be shared by AI and player. First consumer is a rock-throwing ranged enemy; player throwing plugs into the same component later.
